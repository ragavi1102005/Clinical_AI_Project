TYPES OF UTI
UNCOMPLICATED UTI
Infection confined to urinary
bladder in afebrile patients
• At least 3 symptoms - dysuria,
frequency, urgency, suprapubic
pain
OR
• If less than three symptoms:
◦ Dipstick +ve for leukocyte
esterase and nitrite OR
◦ >10 WBC /HPF in centrifuged
sample OR
◦ Urine culture with 105/ml colony
count in mid stream urine
culture
COMPLICATED UTI/PYELONEPHRITIS
Infection extending beyond bladder
or associated with systemic illness.
• Fever
• Flank pain
• Features of sepsis- chills, rigors,
hemodynamic instability
• Costovertebral angle tenderness
• Perineal pain and tenderness
WHEN NOT TO DO URINE CULTURE
All uncomplicated UTIs
Asymtomatic urinary catheters
